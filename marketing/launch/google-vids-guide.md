# Google Vids assembly guide

This kit contains footage and an editable deck, not a finished video. Start with the 120-second landscape introduction. The 28-second portrait edit has its own storyboard. Record your voice after placing the scenes, then retime captions to the performance.

The current PowerPoint embeds seven feature clips on slides 2–8, with automatic-start timing. These work as native media objects in compatible PowerPoint players, with static poster fallbacks. Actual desktop PowerPoint playback remains untested. Embedded videos may not survive conversion to Google Slides, and Slides-to-Vids conversion excludes videos: use the separate MP4s for the Vids timeline below.

## 1. Bring the deck into Slides

Upload `readmory-promo.pptx` to your own Google Drive. Open it with Google Slides. If it remains an Office file, save a native Google Slides copy before conversion. Check the serif headings, line breaks and slide images after conversion. Text, image placement and backgrounds remain editable; screenshots themselves are raster images of the actual app. [Google’s PowerPoint-to-Slides instructions](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/9310378?hl=en).

## 2. Create scenes in Vids

Open the native Slides file and choose **File → Convert to video**, or create a Vid and choose **Convert Slides**. Select the slides needed for the timeline below. Google documents five seconds as the initial duration per imported slide, so adjust those durations. Videos embedded inside Slides are not imported by this conversion. Add the supplied MP4s directly in Vids after importing the layout. Speaker notes contain draft scene narration and sources; remove source notes from any generated voice script. [Slides conversion and limits](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15577408?hl=en).

Use **Upload** to add files from this kit or from Drive. Choose the `-landscape.mp4` variants. Place footage above or in place of the screenshot, preserving its proportions. Use the screenshot as a still hold if a scene outlasts the supplied recording. Keep the editable title, but move it if it covers an important control. [Adding images and clips](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14917464?hl=en).

## 4. Voice, music and captions

Use your own voice for an indie developer introduction. Read `narration.md` scene by scene and record into Vids or upload your voice file. If your account offers generated voiceover, paste only the spoken script and review pronunciation of Readmory, Kobo, KOReader and Kobalt. No generated voice or paid voice service is required by this kit.

Choose a quiet licensed instrumental from your available editor library. Keep its level low under speech, with a short opening fade and a one-second ending fade. The kit bundles no music and makes no claim that a track is licensed for your accounts. Recordings are silent by design. Google lists uploaded audio and video as supported media. [Media compatibility and export](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15082958?hl=en).

For a caption track, use **Tools → Closed captions → + Caption track → Upload file**, choose English, and select the matching SRT after retiming it. Google also supports manual caption entry. Closed captions require playback toggling; for social exports, create visible on-screen caption text as well and check the exported MP4. Do not assume a closed-caption track becomes permanently visible in the export. [Caption tools](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/14953386?hl=en).

## 5. Shorts, Reels and Threads — 28 seconds

Create a separate Vid and select **Video size → Vertical (9:16)**, or **File → Video size**. Google currently documents widescreen, vertical and square sizes. Content may need manual positioning after a size change. [Video size instructions](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/16545758?hl=en).

Follow `short-video-storyboard.md` exactly. Use `-portrait.mp4` clips and the portrait end card. Do not automatically crop the landscape deck into the vertical frame. Recreate the few editable text overlays using `creative-direction.md`; the responsive app clips already provide the vertical visual composition. Keep captions above the platform controls. Use `captions/short.srt` as the timing baseline.

If an account lacks Slides conversion, upload the supplied `slides/slide-NN.png` as scene backgrounds and place MP4s directly on the timeline. That is a documented image/video upload workflow, with text editable only if recreated in Vids. If vertical controls are unavailable in that account, assemble the provided portrait MP4s and end card in an editor that exposes a 1080×1920 canvas. The material needs no new recording, but the final edit remains a manual production step.

## 8. Export and final review

Preview at full size. Check narration timing, captions, source labels, synthetic import labeling and the final URL. Google’s current help lists **File → Make a copy → MP4 (.mp4)** for downloading, and an Export to Drive option. UI labels may differ by rollout. Export your finished Vid, open the downloaded MP4, and confirm the chosen ratio, readable overlays and audible voice. [Current export instructions](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/15082958?hl=en).

Upload the finished MP4 to each platform yourself and use `social-copy.md`. No public posts or final edited video have been produced here. Selected personal footage was uploaded to the private Google Slides production folder and remains and ignored by Git for owner review. Keep raw backups and profiles out of Drive and out of public artifacts.
