I built Readmory because I wanted to come back to more than a list of books I’d read.
It brings Kobo, KOReader and Kobalt reading records into one local workspace. This is my own cleaned library in the app.
I can explore recorded reading time, open a day in the calendar, and see the source behind the history. Estimates stay labeled.
Search brings old highlights back into reach. Personal notes stay separate from the quoted passage, so my own thoughts are easier to revisit.
The core works without an account. Reading data stays in the browser, and portable backups help protect it. Cover lookup is optional.
It’s an independent, open-source public beta. If you use a Kobo or one of these readers, I’d love your feedback.
