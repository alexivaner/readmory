# Creative direction

Readmory — Your reading, remembered.

A quiet personal introduction by Ivan. The footage is the evidence. Use existing book covers and real application interactions, with generous pauses so a viewer can follow one action at a time.

Brand source: `docs/BRANDING.md`. Forest #1B2B22, deep green #2F4A3C, sage #A9CC8A, cream #F7F3E9, warm gray #D2CCC1, charcoal #2B2B2B. Editable serif headings and simple sans-serif supporting text echo the interface. No generated artwork or invented dashboard. Brand icon remains unchanged.

Use 150–250 ms dissolves at scene boundaries. No rotating covers, animated counters or artificial cursor effects. Keep filmed estimate/provenance labels visible. Music is optional: quiet instrumental with no vocals, roughly 70–90 BPM. Choose a licensed track in the editor and keep it comfortably beneath the voice. No audio asset or license is bundled.

Landscape: 1920×1080 UI. Portrait: responsive 540×960 CSS viewport, screenshots at 2× resolution and H.264 clips at 1080×1920. This is a genuine mobile composition, not a compressed desktop screen. Keep overlays in the upper/lower reserved area and move them when they cover a control. Avoid bottom 300 px and rightmost 140 px for critical text in vertical exports; platform overlays vary.
