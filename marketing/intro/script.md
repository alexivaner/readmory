# Readmory introduction

56 seconds · 1920×1080 · English synthetic voice (macOS Samantha)

- **MEET READMORY (7s):** Meet Readmory. A quiet home for your books, your highlights, and the ideas you want to remember.
- **01 / CONNECT (8s):** Import your reading data from Kobo, KOReader, or Kobalt. Review what you are bringing in, then save it to your local library.
- **02 / EXPLORE (7s):** Browse your bookshelf, check your progress, and return to the books you have spent time with.
- **03 / REMEMBER (8s):** Search your highlights and notes to find a thought again, with the book it came from always close by.
- **04 / REFLECT (8s):** Add your own reflections, and keep your personal notes together. Let one good passage become the start of a new idea.
- **05 / DISCOVER (9s):** Explore your reading rhythm through a calendar of available activity. See the source behind each record, with estimates clearly marked.
- **YOUR READING, REMEMBERED. (9s):** Your library stays in your browser, with no account required. Keep a backup, and try the Readmory public beta today.
